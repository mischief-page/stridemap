Privacy

Last updated September 29, 2026

Your health and location data never leave your device. stridemap reads your export and draws your picture entirely inside your web browser. Nothing you load is uploaded, stored, or seen by anyone else, including us. (The optional street map loads map tiles for your area; see below.)

Your workouts

  • When you choose an Apple Health export or a Strava download (or try the sample data), your browser reads it and draws the picture on your own computer or phone. The file is never sent anywhere.
  • Nothing is saved after you close the page.
  • The code is open source, so anyone can check this: github.com/mischief-page/stridemap.

The images you download

  • Every route is drawn from a shared starting point, so the image contains no real map coordinates and can't be used to find where you live.
  • Except with the optional street map. A map shows the real place your routes start from, so an image with a map in it can reveal where that is, such as your home. Leave the map off, or choose a different point, if you'd rather not share that.
  • A very distinctive route could still be recognizable to someone who knows the area, so think about that before sharing.
  • Anything you type into the legend, like a title or your name, becomes part of the image.

The optional street map

  • The map is off unless you turn it on. To draw it, your browser downloads map tiles for the area shown from OpenFreeMap. Like any website, OpenFreeMap sees those requests (your IP address and which tiles, which reveals the area to within a few kilometers). Your routes and workouts are not sent.
  • If you place the map by address and press Find, the address you typed is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim search, under the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy. Nothing is sent while you type, and coordinates you enter yourself aren't sent to anyone for lookup.
  • Finding "where most routes start" is worked out on your device.

Ordering a print

  • Nothing is sent while you design. Previewing a product and ordering are the only times anything leaves your device, and only the finished picture is sent, never your workouts or GPS tracks.
  • Room previews: “See it in a room” sends a small image of the picture to our print shop and to Printful, which makes the scenes.
  • Checkout: the picture goes to our print shop, and you pay on a page run by Stripe, which collects your name, email, shipping address and card. We never see your card.
  • Printing and shipping: Printful receives the print file and your name, address and email, to make the product, ship it, and email you tracking.
  • We keep a record of each order (product, price, email and address) for receipts, problems and tax records. Pictures from checkouts that were never paid are deleted after a day; stored pictures and print files expire after 90 days.
  • If the picture includes the street map, it shows the real place your routes start from, so the print shop and Printful see that place.

What we collect

  • Nothing. There are no accounts, cookies, analytics, ads or tracking scripts.
  • The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which handles basic request information (such as IP addresses) to deliver pages and block abuse, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. We don't add any tracking of our own.

If this changes

If we add anything else that collects information, such as an email list, we'll update this page first and say exactly what's collected and why.
