Making and shipping

  • Each order is made for you, usually within 2–5 business days, then shipped within the US. Most orders arrive within about two weeks of ordering.
  • You'll get a receipt by email when you pay, and tracking by email from Printful when it ships.
  • Prints leave off the small “made with stridemap” mark.
  • Since everything is made to order with your design, we can't take returns for a change of mind. If something arrives damaged or misprinted, contact us within 30 days of delivery and we'll replace it.
